## Planned sections

- **Entity:** the customer or business record. See [Entity 360](/concepts/entity-360).
- **Verification:** a KYC, KYB or AML check run against an entity.
- **Alert:** a signal raised by monitoring. See the [Monitoring Model](/concepts/monitoring-model).
- **Case:** a group of alerts for investigation.
- **Report:** a regulatory or internal output built from the above.
