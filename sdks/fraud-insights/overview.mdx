The Fraud Insights SDK collects passive device and behavioural signals during a session. These signals enrich the risk score computed by the Youverify platform without adding friction for the user.

## Works on every platform

Fraud Insights runs in the background, so it is not tied to a single platform. It works the same way on web, Android and iOS, and there are no separate platform guides to follow. Your users don't see or interact with anything.

<Info>
  Because Fraud Insights runs in the background, it adds no screens or prompts to your verification flows. It works alongside the [Anti-Deepfake Liveness](/sdks/liveness/overview) and [Document Capture](/sdks/document-capture/overview) SDKs.
</Info>

To learn how risk scores are calculated, see [Risk scoring](/concepts/risk-scoring). For setup help, contact [support@youverify.co](mailto:support@youverify.co).
