Quick answers to common questions about the Cowork dashboard, with links to the full guides.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is the difference between Sandbox and Live?">
    Sandbox is for testing. Live runs real verifications. API keys are created separately for each environment, so a Sandbox key does not work in Live. To unlock Live, [activate your business account](/how-to/activate-business-account). To create keys, see [API keys](/how-to/api-keys).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which verification types can I run?">
    Cowork supports identity checks such as NIN, BVN and passport, address verification, and AML screening. Coverage depends on the country. See [Country and product coverage](/resources/coverage).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I add money to my wallet?">
    Use a card, or transfer to the virtual account assigned to your workspace. See [Fund your wallet](/how-to/fund-wallet).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I know when my balance is running low?">
    Set a threshold and the people you choose get an email when your balance falls below it. See [Set low balance alerts](/how-to/low-balance-alerts).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where can I see what I have spent and download invoices?">
    On the Billing & Wallets page. See [Billing, wallets and invoices](/how-to/billing-wallets-invoices).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I invite a colleague, and what can they do?">
    Invite them from Team Management and give them a role. See [Manage team members](/how-to/manage-team-members) and [User roles and permissions](/how-to/user-roles).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I make my login more secure?">
    Turn on two-factor authentication in Account Settings. See [Two-factor authentication](/how-to/two-factor-authentication).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What do the status labels on my checks mean?">
    See [Verification statuses](/how-to/verification-statuses). For address checks, see [Address verification report statuses](/how-to/address-report-statuses).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

Can't find your answer? Contact [support@youverify.co](mailto:support@youverify.co).
