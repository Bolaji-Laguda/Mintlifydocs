---

Entities are the people and businesses you do business with. Each entity has a profile with its risk score, linked checks and screening activity, so you can monitor risk over time. See [Entity 360](/concepts/entity-360) to learn more.

There are three entity types: **Individual**, **Business** and **UBOs** (ultimate beneficial owners).

## Create an entity

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Entities">
    Click **Entities** in the left sidebar.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/how-to/manage-entities/step-1.png" alt="Entities in the left sidebar" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the entity type">
    Select the **Individual**, **Business** or **UBOs** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a new request">
    Click **New Request** at the top right.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/how-to/manage-entities/step-3.png" alt="New Request button on the Entities page" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the profile">
    Complete the mandatory profile fields. Typical fields include name, registration number, date of birth, ID type, tax ID and ownership percentage, depending on the entity type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign risk parameters and save">
    Assign the risk parameters, then click **Save Entity**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## View and manage an entity

Open any entity to see its details in the side drawer: its risk score, linked checks and screening activity.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/how-to/manage-entities/drawer.png" alt="Entity drawer showing risk score, linked checks and screening activity" />
</Frame>

To learn how scores are calculated, see [Risk scoring](/concepts/risk-scoring).
