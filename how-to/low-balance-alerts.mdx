sidebarTitle: "Low balance alerts"

---

Set a threshold and Cowork emails the recipients you choose when your wallet balance falls below it, so you can top up before checks start failing.

## Set up an alert

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing & Wallets">
    Click your **Profile icon**, then select **Billing & Wallets**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Balance Alerts">
    Click **Balance Alerts**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/how-to/low-balance-alerts/step-2.png" alt="Balance Alerts option on the Billing and Wallets page" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on the notification">
    Enable **Low Balance Notification**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the threshold and recipients">
    Enter the threshold amount, for example N50,000, and the email addresses that should receive the alert.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/how-to/low-balance-alerts/step-4.png" alt="Low balance alert form with a threshold amount and recipient emails" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Preferences**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
