Two-factor authentication (2FA) asks for a one-time code from an authenticator app each time you sign in to Cowork, on top of your password.

## Turn on 2FA

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Account Settings">
    Sign in to Cowork. Click your **Profile icon**, then select **Account Settings**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/how-to/two-factor-authentication/step-1.png" alt="Account Settings opened from the profile menu" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open System Settings">
    Click **System Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable 2FA for login">
    Turn on the toggle to enable 2FA for login, then click **Activate 2FA**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/how-to/two-factor-authentication/step-3.png" alt="Toggle to enable 2FA for login and the Activate 2FA button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link your authenticator app">
    Scan the QR code shown on screen with your authenticator app. If you can't scan it, enter the setup key in the app instead.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/how-to/two-factor-authentication/step-4.png" alt="QR code and setup key shown for authenticator app enrolment" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm with a one-time code">
    Enter the one-time code from your authenticator app in Cowork to finish enrolment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Store your recovery codes">
    If Cowork shows recovery codes, store them somewhere safe. You may need them if you lose access to your authenticator app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Only enter the setup key in your authenticator app. Never share it, or your one-time codes, with anyone.
</Warning>
