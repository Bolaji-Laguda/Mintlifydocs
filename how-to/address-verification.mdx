Address verification confirms where a candidate or business is located. You can submit a single request, or upload many at once with a CSV template.

There are two check types:

- **Physical Field Visit:** a field agent visits the address.
- **Digital Geolocation:** the address is checked digitally.

## Submit a request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Address Verification">
    Go to **Quick Check** > **Address Verification**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/how-to/address-verification/step-1.png" alt="Address Verification under Quick Check" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a new check">
    Click **+ New Address Check**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the check type">
    Select **Physical Field Visit** or **Digital Geolocation**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/how-to/address-verification/step-3.png" alt="Check type selection with Physical Field Visit and Digital Geolocation" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose single entry or CSV upload">
    Submit one candidate at a time, or upload several with the CSV template.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the details">
    Provide the candidate or entity details: residential address, state, LGA, landmark and phone number. Confirm that you have the candidate's consent.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/how-to/address-verification/step-5.png" alt="Address details form with state, LGA, landmark and phone number" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Click **Submit Request**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After you submit, follow the progress in [Track address verification](/how-to/track-address-verification).
