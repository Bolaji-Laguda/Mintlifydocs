You can fund your Cowork wallet with a debit or credit card, or by bank transfer to the virtual account assigned to your workspace.

## Open the funding options

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing & Wallets">
    Click your **Profile icon**, then select **Billing & Wallets**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Fund Wallet">
    Click **Fund Wallet**, then choose how you want to pay.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/how-to/fund-wallet/step-2.png" alt="Fund Wallet dialog with Card Payment and Bank Transfer options" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Choose a payment method

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Card">
    Your balance updates instantly when the payment completes.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Select Card Payment">
        Choose the **Card Payment** method.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter the amount and authorise">
        Enter the top-up amount and complete the 3DS authorisation.

        <Frame>
          <img src="/images/how-to/fund-wallet/card-payment.png" alt="Card payment form with the top-up amount" />
        </Frame>
      </Step>
      <Step title="Check your balance">
        Your wallet balance updates as soon as the payment completes.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Bank transfer">
    Each workspace has a dedicated virtual account. Transfers to it are credited to your wallet.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Select Bank Transfer">
        Choose **Bank Transfer**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Copy the account details">
        Copy the virtual account details, including the bank name and account number.

        <Frame>
          <img src="/images/how-to/fund-wallet/bank-transfer.png" alt="Virtual account details with bank name and account number" />
        </Frame>
      </Step>
      <Step title="Make the transfer">
        Transfer the funds from your bank's portal or your corporate banking app.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Confirm the credit">
        Once the transfer is received, the confirmation appears under **Billing History**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

To get warned when your balance is low, see [Set low balance alerts](/how-to/low-balance-alerts).
