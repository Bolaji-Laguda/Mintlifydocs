Use Address History to follow each request while it moves through verification and to see the agent's field report when it is done.

## Check the status of a request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Address History">
    Go to **Quick Check** > **Address History**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/how-to/track-address-verification/step-1.png" alt="Address History under Quick Check" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the request">
    Filter by candidate name, reference ID or date range.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the record">
    Click the record to open the side drawer. It shows the agent's notes, GPS coordinates and photo proof.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/how-to/track-address-verification/step-3.png" alt="Record drawer with agent notes, GPS coordinates and photo proof" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

To understand each status, see [Address verification report statuses](/how-to/address-report-statuses).
