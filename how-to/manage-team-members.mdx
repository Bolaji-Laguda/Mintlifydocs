---
title: "Invite, manage and remove team members"
sidebarTitle: "Manage team members"
description: "Invite people to your Cowork workspace, give them the right role, and remove their access when they leave."
---

Manage who can access your Cowork workspace by inviting team members, assigning each person a role, and removing access when it is no longer needed. Roles control what each person can do. See [User roles and permissions](/how-to/user-roles).

<Info>
  You need an account that is authorised to manage workspace users. The Owner role has full control over team management.
</Info>

## Invite a team member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Team Management">
    Click your **Profile icon**, then select **Team Management** from the sidebar.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/how-to/manage-team-members/step-1.png" alt="Team Management page opened from the profile menu" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start an invitation">
    Click **+ Invite User** at the top right.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/how-to/manage-team-members/step-2.png" alt="The Invite User button at the top right of Team Management" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the email and choose a role">
    Enter the person's corporate email address and select a role: Owner, Admin, Analyst or Viewer. Assign only the role the person needs.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/how-to/manage-team-members/step-3.png" alt="Invite User form with an email field and a role selector" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invite">
    Review the details and click **Send Invite**. Ask the invitee to follow the invitation email and complete any account setup steps.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Change or remove access

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the user">
    In **Team Management**, locate the person in the user list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the actions menu">
    Click the actions menu (**...**) next to their name.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/how-to/manage-team-members/step-5.png" alt="Actions menu next to a user in the team list" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage or revoke access">
    To remove someone, choose the action that deactivates their access, review the confirmation prompt, and confirm only if you are authorised to do so.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Removing a user's access takes effect for their account on your workspace. Confirm that you are removing the right person before you confirm.
</Warning>
