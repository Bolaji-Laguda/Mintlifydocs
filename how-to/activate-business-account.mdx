Activating your business account unlocks production features and live verifications. Until you activate, you can keep testing in the sandbox. You complete your business profile, upload your documents, fund your wallet and submit the account for compliance review.

## Before you start

Have these ready:

- Your business name, registration number (RC or BN), tax identification number (TIN), address and sector.
- Your incorporation documents.
- Identity documents for your directors.

## Activate your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your business profile">
    Log in to Cowork and go to **Settings** > **Business Profile**. You can also start from the activation banner on the **Overview** page.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/how-to/activate-business-account/step-1.png" alt="Business Profile page under Settings" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your business details">
    Enter your business name, registration number (RC or BN), TIN, address and sector.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/how-to/activate-business-account/step-2.png" alt="Business profile form with registration details" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your documents">
    Upload your incorporation documents and the identity documents of your directors and UBOs.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/how-to/activate-business-account/step-3.png" alt="Document upload section for incorporation documents and director IDs" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fund your wallet">
    Add funds to your wallet. See [Fund your wallet](/how-to/fund-wallet).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit for review">
    Click **Submit Activation Request**. Your request goes to Youverify for compliance review.
  </Step>
</Steps>
