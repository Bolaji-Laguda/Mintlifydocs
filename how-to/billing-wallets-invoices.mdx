The **Billing & Wallets** page shows your wallet balance, what you have spent on each service, your recent transactions, and your invoices.

## View your balance and usage

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing & Wallets">
    Click your **Profile icon**, then select **Billing & Wallets**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/how-to/billing-wallets-invoices/step-1.png" alt="Billing and Wallets opened from the profile menu" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your account">
    View your **Current Balance**, **Usage Breakdown** and **Recent Transactions**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/how-to/billing-wallets-invoices/step-2.png" alt="Billing page showing current balance, usage breakdown and recent transactions" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download an invoice">
    In the **Billing History** table, download the PDF invoice you need.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To add money to your wallet, see [Fund your wallet](/how-to/fund-wallet). To get warned before your balance runs out, see [Set low balance alerts](/how-to/low-balance-alerts).
